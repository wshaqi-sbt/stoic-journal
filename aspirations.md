# Aspirations

Aspirations are not goals. They have no deadline, threshold or boxes to tick. They are things important enough to decide what I do. They work as a criterion: does what I do or plan today serve them, or contradict them?

Each aspiration separates what is my task from what does not depend on me. An aspiration is in my control as a direction of action, never as an outcome.

Changing an aspiration is not a failure. It is a journal entry with a date.
